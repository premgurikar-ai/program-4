Steve Jobs was one of the most influential innovators in the field of technology. His full name was Steven Paul Jobs, and he was born on February 24, 1955, in San Francisco. He was adopted as a child and showed a keen interest in electronics from a young age. Although he joined college, he later dropped out to follow his passion.

In 1976, Steve Jobs co-founded Apple Inc. in his parents’ garage along with Steve Wozniak. This marked the beginning of a revolutionary journey in the world of computers and technology. He played a key role in creating innovative products like the iPhone, iPad, iPod, and MacBook, which changed the way people use technology.

Steve Jobs also founded Pixar Animation Studios, which produced famous animated films such as *Toy Story* and *Finding Nemo*. Despite his success, he faced many challenges in life. He was even fired from Apple, the company he started, but he did not give up. Later, he returned to Apple and transformed it into one of the world’s leading technology companies.

Jobs was known for his creativity, determination, visionary thinking, and willingness to take risks. Even though he faced failures and serious health issues, he continued to inspire millions of people. His famous quote, “Stay hungry, stay foolish,” encourages people to keep learning and striving for success.

Steve Jobs passed away on October 5, 2011, but his legacy continues to inspire people around the world. His life teaches us to follow our passion, think differently, and never give up in the face of difficulties.
